> **What if sales are increasing, but profitability isn't?**
> That question drove me to look beyond basic sales numbers and turn raw Superstore transaction data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using **Microsoft Excel**.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw transactional data from a Superstore dataset into an interactive Excel dashboard. The goal was not just to build charts, but to answer real business questions:

- Where are sales coming from?
- Which categories contribute the most?
- Which customer segments drive revenue?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which products are performing strongly?
- Do high sales necessarily translate into high profitability?

## 📊 Dashboard Components

- **KPI Cards:** Total sales, total profit, total orders, average sales, average profit, max and min sales
- **Region Wise Sales:** Column chart comparing Central, East, South and West
- **Category Wise Analysis:** Pie chart of sales share by category
- **State Wise Sales:** Bar chart of sales by state
- **Profit by Category:** Donut chart of profit contribution
- **Segment Wise Sales:** Pie chart of Consumer, Corporate and Home Office
- **Sales by Year:** Line chart showing the sales trend over time
- **Top 10 Products:** Bar chart of best-selling products
- **Slicers:** Filter the entire dashboard by Region, Segment and Category

## 💡 Key Business Insights

### 🌍 Regional Performance
The **West** region generated the highest sales, while the **South** showed comparatively lower sales, highlighting an opportunity to investigate regional performance drivers.

### 🗂️ Category Analysis
**Technology** contributed the largest share of sales (37%), followed by **Furniture** (32%) and **Office Supplies** (31%).

### 💰 Profitability
Sales contribution and profit contribution differ significantly across categories:

| Category | Share of Sales | Share of Profit |
|----------|:--------------:|:---------------:|
| Technology | 37% | 51% |
| Office Supplies | 31% | 43% |
| Furniture | 32% | 6% |

Furniture brings in nearly a third of sales but only about 6% of profit, which is a good reminder to monitor **profitability, not revenue alone**.

### 👥 Customer Segments
The **Consumer** segment accounted for the largest share of sales (50%), followed by **Corporate** (31%) and **Home Office** (19%).

### 📅 Sales Trend
The dashboard shows an overall **upward sales trend** across the analyzed years, creating an opportunity to investigate the key factors behind this growth.

### 🏆 Top Products
Identifying the highest-selling products can support inventory planning, product strategy and targeted marketing decisions.

---

## ✅ Business Recommendations

1. Investigate the factors driving strong performance in high-performing regions.
2. Analyze opportunities to improve underperforming regions.
3. Focus on products that combine high sales with strong profitability.
4. Review discounting strategies to understand their impact on margins.
5. Develop targeted strategies for different customer segments.
6. Use top-product trends to support inventory and marketing decisions.
7. Continue monitoring sales and profit growth together rather than focusing on revenue alone.
